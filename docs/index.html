Limitations — read this before quoting any number

  • Settlement is not execution. Every price here is an exchange settlement print, not a quote you could have lifted. The executability audit upper-bounds what was reachable; it does not establish a fill.
  • Minting cost is assumed, not measured. ₹20–60 per bar. Conclusions are claimed only where they survive that whole range — see the sensitivity table.
  • The duty wedge is carried as a range. The 995/999 purity part is exact (999/995); the duty-basis part is policy and is stated, never fitted.
  • Regime breaks are dated, not always explained. A discontinuity is reported as an event. Where we tested an explanation (the May 2026 duty hike) and it failed the timing test, we say so rather than inventing a cause.
  • Input coverage is limited by what MCX listed. GOLDTEN has no history before April 2025, so any GOLDTEN-based series is shorter than the headline window.
