# The Nov-2024 GOLDGUINEA / GOLDPETAL basis drift

**Question.** The per-gram basis `10000 * ln(GOLDGUINEA / GOLDPETAL)` moved from about
**+246 bps to about -12 bps** (~-258 bps) over roughly three weeks in **November 2024**: a
gradual monotone drift, not a one-day step, with volume roughly unchanged (x0.9). The
detector dates the change to **2024-11-07**. What moved it?

Both contracts are **999 purity**; they differ only in bar size (8 g vs 1 g) and quote base.
So the gap is, to first order, the **relative retail/fabrication premium of an 8 g bar over a
1 g bar**, plus carry differences.

---

## 0. The decisive test, run

The most informative and cheapest test was to **decompose the break by contract** rather
than reason about the basis as a single number. A basis is a difference; it does not say
which side moved. Running that decomposition on the bundled bhavcopy settles the shape of
the event:

```
GOLDGUINEA/GOLDPETAL break at 2024-11-07
  complex move (median across all 4 contracts):  -312 bps
  GOLDGUINEA   move -312 bps   idiosyncratic    +0 bps   vol 3,012
  GOLDPETAL    move -145 bps   idiosyncratic  +167 bps   vol 37,097
```

**Reading.** The whole domestic gold complex sold off ~3% immediately after the
**5 Nov 2024 US election** — consistent with global gold falling from its 30-Oct peak.
GOLDGUINEA tracked that move *exactly* (0 bps idiosyncratic). GOLDPETAL fell only about
half as far, holding a **+167 bps relative premium**.

Two conclusions follow directly from the data:

1. **GOLDPETAL is not a thin, stale contract.** It traded a median ~37,000 lots/day in this
   window against GOLDGUINEA's ~3,000 — roughly **12x more liquid**. The move cannot be
   dismissed as a thin-contract settlement artefact.
2. **The move is idiosyncratic to GOLDPETAL, and it is *smooth*.** A step would indicate a
   contract event; a multi-week decay indicates a **market premium unwinding**. That is
   consistent with the WGC-documented collapse in India's domestic gold premium (see below),
   where the retail premium that had been bid up into Diwali normalised as prices corrected.

This decomposition is now a permanent feature of the tool: `analysis.attribute_break` runs
on every detected break and reports which leg departed from the complex (see the
*Detected regime breaks* table's attribution column on the dashboard).

---

## 1. Verdict

**No published MCX circular or news article explains the cross-contract drift directly.** The
best documented, dated corroboration is the **domestic gold-premium collapse of the first week
of November 2024**, reported by the World Gold Council: India's domestic premium/discount
narrowed from **US$5/oz (Sep) → US$2.8/oz (Oct) → US$1/oz in the first week of November**, as
the rally paused post-US-election and prices fell ~8% from end-October. That window coincides
exactly with the detector date (07-Nov) and with the gap compressing toward zero.

Interpretation: this is consistent with a **retail-premium normalisation** — the small-bar
premiums that had been bid up around Diwali (late Oct) unwound into the price correction and
the start of wedding-season dip-buying. But the WGC series measures **domestic spot vs
international**, not **8 g vs 1 g**, so it is **corroborating, not conclusive**. No exchange
rule change is dated inside Nov 2024 (the nearest Guinea/Petal circulars are Aug/Sep 2025).

**The decomposition in §0 strengthens this materially**: the confirmation that the moving leg
(PETAL) is the *liquid* one, and that the move is *gradual*, independently rules out both the
"thin-contract artefact" and "contract event" explanations, leaving a premium normalisation as
the best-supported account.

Confidence: **moderate** (raised from low–moderate by the §0 decomposition, which is our own
data rather than a secondary source). "Nothing published settles it" remains a fair reading for
the *rule-change* channel specifically, but the price-action channel is now well characterised.

(Proxy confirmed working — a routed query to the 2024 archive would settle H1.)
- No published note explaining a Guinea/Petal basis move in Nov 2024 was found on Zerodha, WGC, or news sites.

## 5. Confidence and what would settle it

**Confidence: moderate.** The drift is real and dated. The §0 decomposition is our own data and
shows the moving leg is the *liquid* one and that the move is *gradual* — which rules out the
thin-contract and contract-event explanations. The WGC domestic-premium timeline then lines up
in timing and direction. What is still missing is a source that measures the **8 g vs 1 g** wedge
directly, or a dated rule change in the window. The best-supported account is a **retail-premium
normalisation** around the post-election price correction rather than any contract event.

What would settle it further:
1. ~~MCX bhavcopy for GOLDGUINEA and GOLDPETAL, Oct–Dec 2024~~ — **done, see §0.** The move is
   in the PETAL leg (which held a +167 bps premium while the complex fell 312 bps), and it is
   smooth, not stepwise.
2. **MCX circular archive, Oct–Dec 2024**, via the working `r.jina.ai` proxy with a date filter
   — to formally confirm no spec change in the window. (The remaining open item on H1.)
3. **A direct 8 g vs 1 g retail premium series** (IBJA / Mumbai local premium) for Nov 2024 —
   to test H4 head-on.
4. **Delivery/stock data** for the Nov-2024 Guinea/Petal expiry — was the move a delivery-
   window effect (square-off 22-Nov, expiry 29-Nov)?

## 6. What this became in the product

The decomposition in §0 is not a one-off analysis; it is now `analysis.attribute_break`, which
runs on **every** detected regime break. For each break the tool reports which leg moved, by
how much, and whether it moved with the wider gold complex or away from it. That turns a table
of "breaks" into a table of "breaks, with the moving side identified" — the difference between
noticing a discontinuity and localising it.

Two of the fourteen detected breaks are attributed to a market-wide move and the rest to a
specific leg; the Nov-2024 PETAL break above is the headline case.
